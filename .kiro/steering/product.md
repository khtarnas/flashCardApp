# Product Overview – HanaHou

## What is HanaHou?

HanaHou ("one more time" / "encore" in Hawaiian) is a personal iPadOS flashcard app designed for language learning. It prioritizes a clean, offline-first study experience with gradual layering of advanced features.

## Who is it for?

- **Primary user:** Hokua Tarnas (solo developer and sole user).
- **Use case:** Personal language study — reviewing vocabulary, phrases, and concepts across multiple languages.
- **Context:** Focused study sessions on iPad, likely at a desk.

## What problem does it solve?

Existing flashcard apps (Anki, Quizlet, etc.) are either:
- Too complex and ugly (Anki)
- Too simple and cloud-dependent (Quizlet)
- Missing Apple Pencil integration for handwriting practice

HanaHou aims to be a high-quality, offline-first flashcard experience that feels native to iPad, with Apple Pencil support for writing practice — something no major flashcard app does well.

## Core value proposition

A beautiful, offline-first flashcard app for iPad that grows with the learner — starting with simple deck/card management and study mode, then adding handwriting (Apple Pencil), spaced repetition, and AI-powered features over time.

## Success criteria

- The app is genuinely useful for daily language study.
- Study mode feels smooth and distraction-free.
- Cards and decks are easy to create and manage.
- The app works completely offline.
- Apple Pencil integration feels natural (future priority).

## Product Philosophy

Guiding beliefs that shape design and prioritization decisions. These aren't features — they're values.

- **Data about your learning is inspiring.** Statistics and progress visualization should be prominent, not hidden. Seeing how much you've learned motivates you to keep going.
- **Everything should be mutable.** Modularity and separation of concerns above all. Every component should be swappable, extensible, or reconfigurable without rewriting.
- **Grow with the learner.** Start simple, layer complexity gradually. The app should feel useful on day one and more powerful on day one hundred.
- **Nothing is sacred — but only with user approval.** Every decision and convention can be revisited, but only when the user initiates or approves the change. The agent follows recorded decisions until told otherwise.

## What HanaHou is NOT

- Not a social/sharing platform.
- Not a multi-user app (no accounts, no sync in early priorities).
- Not a general-purpose note-taking app.
- Not trying to replace Anki for power users — it's a personal tool first.
